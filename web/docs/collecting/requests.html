Asking for the data

Most of the work of a first report is asking other people for data: billing for coverage and visits, the EHR team for PHQ results, whoever keeps the registry for the CoCM records. These requests are ready to send. Copy one, fill in the parts in [brackets], and attach or link the CSV templates, so the reply comes back in the shape the calculator reads.

Before sending any of them:

  • Agree how files will come back. Every report below contains patient information. Ask for it through your organization's secure file share or secure email, not ordinary email.
  • Ask for the MRN as the patient id in every report, so the reports join. If the tables will leave your site, pseudonymize them with your site's key first (see one patient id).
  • Ask once, then ask for the same report every month. The first request takes the most explaining; after that it's "the same as last month, for [month]".

Which requests a site needs depends on its route (collecting the data):

RequestWho usually has itEvery site?
CoverageBilling or practice managementYes
Practice visitsBilling or practice managementYes
PHQ resultsThe EHR team or a report writerYes
CoCM recordsWhoever keeps the registry, or the EHR team if there's no registryYes, unless the program lead fills in the workbook from the registry themselves
Program factsThe program leadYes

Coverage

Subject: Coverage report for CoCM reporting, [month and year]

Hi [name],

For our Collaborative Care report to the NYS Office of Mental Health, I need each patient's insurance coverage, with dates. Could you run a report of every coverage period that was active on any day of [month and year], for the patients in the attached list (or for all patients seen in [month and year], if that's easier)?

One row per coverage period, with these columns:

  • patient_id: the MRN
  • payer_category: one of medicaid_ffs, medicaid_managed_care, dual_medicare_medicaid, child_health_plus, essential_plan, medicare, commercial, self_pay, other
  • start_date and end_date: the period's effective dates, as YYYY-MM-DD (blank end date if still active)
  • plan_name: the plan as it appears in billing

Please include every period, not just the current one, and secondary coverage too. If mapping plans to the categories is hard, send the plan names and I'll map them.

Please send it as coverage.csv (the template is attached) through [our secure file share]. Thank you!

The payer mapping behind payer_category is a one-time job; see Medicaid.

Practice visits

Subject: Visits report for CoCM reporting, [month and year]

Hi [name],

For our Collaborative Care report to the NYS Office of Mental Health, I need every visit at [practice] from [first day] to [last day of the month], all payers. It's the denominator for the depression screening rate.

One row per visit, with these columns:

  • visit_id: the encounter or visit number
  • patient_id: the MRN
  • visit_date: as YYYY-MM-DD
  • provider_category: primary_care, other_medical, behavioral_health, nursing_only or ancillary, from the rendering provider's type or specialty
  • billing_codes: the CPT and HCPCS codes billed for the visit, separated by ;
  • modality: in_person, video or phone

Please include every visit, not only office visits. The reporting tool works out which ones count. And please send each patient's date of birth too, as patient.csv with patient_id and birth_date.

Please send them as practice_visit.csv and patient.csv (templates attached) through [our secure file share]. Thank you!

PHQ results

Subject: PHQ-2 and PHQ-9 results for CoCM reporting, [month and year]

Hi [name],

For our Collaborative Care report to the NYS Office of Mental Health, I need every scored PHQ-2, PHQ-9 and PHQ-A (the adolescent PHQ-9) recorded for any patient in the 13 months ending [last day of the month], from every department and every questionnaire or flowsheet that holds one, including the patient portal.

One row per scored result, with these columns:

  • result_id: the result or observation id
  • patient_id: the MRN
  • administered_date: when it was completed, as YYYY-MM-DD in local time
  • instrument: phq2, phq9 or phq_a
  • total_score: the total score (leave out declined or unscored forms)

If our EHR can produce a FHIR export of these as Observations instead, that works too.

Please send it as scale_result.csv (template attached) through [our secure file share]. Thank you!

The 13 months cover both screening metrics: 12 months for the screening rate, and the 365 days before each PHQ-9 for the yield. A FHIR export of patients, coverage, encounters and observations can replace the first three requests; see reading FHIR.

CoCM records

Subject: Collaborative Care records for CoCM reporting, [month and year]

Hi [name],

For our Collaborative Care report to the NYS Office of Mental Health, I need the CoCM records for every patient enrolled at any time in [month and year], and for any enrollment still open, however old.

I need four lists, each with the MRN as patient_id and dates as YYYY-MM-DD:

  1. Enrollments (cocm_episode.csv): episode id, enrollment date (the date of the BHCM's initial assessment, not the referral), primary condition, primary scale, discharge date and reason.
  2. Contacts (contact.csv): every contact and outreach attempt since each enrollment began: date, who made it (bhcm or other), what kind (treatment, outreach_attempt, scheduling, no_show, message), and in person, video or phone.
  3. Symptom scales (scale_result.csv): every scored PHQ-9, GAD-7, PCL-5, SMFQ, SCARED, PSC-17 and Vanderbilt since each enrollment began, with the form (child, parent, teacher) where there is one.
  4. Psychiatric case reviews (psych_review.csv): every patient the psychiatric consultant reviewed in the 60 days ending [last day of the month], one row per patient, with whether a recommendation was documented.

The attached templates list every column and the values each one allows.

Please send them through [our secure file share]. Thank you!

If the registry is a spreadsheet the program lead keeps, it's usually quicker for them to fill in the workbook than to answer this request.

Program facts

These don't need a request to anyone. The program lead fills in one row of site_month.csv, or the site_month tab of the workbook:

  • the reporting month;
  • BHCM FTE for the month (BHCM FTE);
  • the screening age floor the practice uses;
  • one sentence on how contacts were judged to be treatment;
  • the date the data was pulled, and the system it came from.

When the files come back

Add them all to the calculator page. Its data check lists any rows it can't use, by id, and anything that looks wrong. Send those lists back to whoever ran the report: they're usually a quick fix once someone can see the rows.
