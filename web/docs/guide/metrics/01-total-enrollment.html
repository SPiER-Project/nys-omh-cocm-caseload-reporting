1. Total enrollment

Total number of patients enrolled in CoCM this month, all payers. (Optimal Caseload: >75 patients / 1 BHCM FTE)

— NYS, metric 1

Reported as: a count. Population: CoCM, all payers.

The rule

Count every patient enrolled this month, whatever their coverage: their enrollment overlaps the month by at least one day. Count each patient once, even if they had two episodes in the month.

The optimal caseload is a reference, not part of the metric. Divide the count by metric 11 to compare.

Worked example

Metric 1: 9.

Where the data lives

Enrollment is in the registry's enrollment or episode records, or in the EHR's program-enrollment records. See enrollment and discharge.

Common mistakes

MistakeEffect
Applying the Medicaid filterUnderstated; that's metric 2
Counting referrals, waitlisted or "planned" patientsOverstated
Leaving disengaged patients enrolled indefinitelyOverstated, and the contact rate falls
Counting only patients enrolled on the last dayUnderstated; patients discharged mid-month drop out

For automation

For developers building their own report. Not needed to report.

The calculator reads cocm_episode, and contact for the inactivity discharge. It counts distinct patient_id values among the episodes that overlap the month.
