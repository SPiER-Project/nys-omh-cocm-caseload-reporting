5. Monthly contact rate

Proportion (%) of Medicaid patients receiving active treatment in CoCM. Active treatment defined as patients who have had at least one clinical contact and symptom-monitoring scale completed this month. […] (2025 Target Rate: ≥ 80%)

— NYS, metric 5, which also defines a clinical contact and lists the scales

Reported as: a percentage. Target: 80% or more. Population: CoCM, Medicaid.

The rule

  • Denominator: metric 2, every Medicaid patient enrolled this month.
  • Numerator: those in active treatment this month. That means both of these, not necessarily on the same day:
    • a clinical contact, which is a live session in which treatment was delivered and documented; outreach attempts, scheduling calls and messages don't count;
    • a scored scale, on any of the seven NYS scales, dated on or after the enrollment date.

A patient enrolled or discharged partway through the month is in the denominator, and needs both within the days they were enrolled.

Where the data lives

Contacts from the registry's contact log, or BHCM and telephone encounters in the EHR. Scales from the registry's scale table, or questionnaires and flowsheets in the EHR. See contacts and reviews.
