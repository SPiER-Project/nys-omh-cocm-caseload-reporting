This guide tells a New York clinic how to report the eleven CoCM metrics NYS OMH asks for each month, and how to get each fact out of the systems it has. It's an opinionated approach to the NYS reporting guidelines. Where turning the guidelines into numbers needs a decision, the decision is recorded as a call, with the reasoning for it.

New to the program itself? About the NYS Collaborative Care Medicaid Program covers what it is, how it began and what it asks of practices, and NYS Medicaid billing covers how certified practices bill for it.

Getting the data

Asking for the data has ready-to-send requests for the people who hold it: billing, the EHR team and whoever keeps the registry.

Collecting the data explains where each fact is kept in a clinic's systems, and gives three routes for getting it out:

  • a spreadsheet registry;
  • a registry product plus an EHR report;
  • the EHR alone.

Before the first report, work through site setup.
